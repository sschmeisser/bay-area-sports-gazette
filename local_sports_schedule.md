* **Fall (Sept – Nov):**
  * *Football:* 49ers (NFL), SJSU, Stanford, Cal, SJCC, De Anza, Branham High, Serra, De La Salle, St. Francis, Leigh, Westmont, Bellarmine, Valley Christian, Mitty.
  * *Soccer:* SJ Earthquakes (MLS stretch run), Bay FC (NWSL), Monterey Bay FC (USL), Oakland Roots (USL), SJSU, SCU, Stanford, CSUMB, West Valley, De Anza.

* **Winter (Dec – Feb):**
  * *Hockey:* San Jose Sharks (NHL), San Jose Barracuda (AHL).
  * *Basketball:* Golden State Warriors (NBA), SJSU Spartans, Santa Clara Broncos, Stanford Cardinal, Cal Bears, Saint Mary's Gaels, CSUMB Otters, SJCC Jaguars, West Valley Vikings, De Anza Mountain Lions, Branham High Boys & Girls.
  * *High School Winter Sports:* Branham Boys & Girls Soccer, High School Basketball.
* **Spring (March – May):**
  * *Baseball & Softball:* SF Giants (MLB), San Jose Giants (Single-A MiLB), Oakland Ballers (Pioneer League), SJSU, SCU, Stanford, CSUMB, West Valley, De Anza, Branham High.

  * *College & Club:* Cal Men's Rugby, San Jose Seahawks Rugby, Oakland Spiders (Ultimate Frisbee).

## Local Sports Fan Tips & Planning Hacks

1. **High School Football Friday Nights:**
   * Tickets for Branham High, Serra, De La Salle, and WCAL schools are almost entirely digital now. Download the **GoFan** app or check the team's MaxPreps page before heading to the gate.
   * Branham home games at Branham Stadium (1570 Branham Ln) kick off at 7:00 PM; arrive by 6:15 PM for good bleacher seating and easy parking.
2. **Almaden FC Summer Matches:**
   * Almaden FC's pre-professional USL2 home games take place at Pioneer High School Stadium on Blossom Hill Rd from May through July. Matches are family-friendly, intimate, and a great way to see top college prospects playing summer ball right in South San Jose.
3. **Public Transit to Games:**
   * **Levi's Stadium (49ers):** Take the VTA Light Rail (Orange or Green Line) directly to the Great America Station right at the stadium gates.
   * **SAP Center (Sharks) & Tech CU Arena (Barracuda):** Diridon Station is directly across the street from SAP Center (Caltrain, Amtrak Capitol Corridor, VTA Light Rail).
   * **Excite Ballpark (SJ Giants) & CEFCU Stadium (SJSU Football):** Located on S 10th St & Alma Ave; easily accessible via Alma exit off Almaden Expy or Hwy 87.
   * **Oakland Roots & Ballers:** Coliseum Station (BART) drops off right at Oakland Coliseum; West Oakland BART is a short ride from Raimondi Park.
   * **Cardinale Stadium (Monterey Bay FC):** Located on the CSUMB campus in Seaside just off Highway 1. Easy parking on campus lots adjacent to 2nd Avenue.
